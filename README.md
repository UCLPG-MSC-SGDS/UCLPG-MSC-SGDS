# Dr Anwar Musah

**Lecturer in Social and Geographic Data Science**
Department of Geography, University College London

I use statistical modelling, geospatial analysis and data science to study questions in epidemiology, disaster risk reduction and the social sciences, with a particular interest in the Global South. I teach quantitative methods and spatial analysis through practical work in R.

[UCL profile](https://profiles.ucl.ac.uk/66048-anwar-musah) · [ORCID](https://orcid.org/0000-0001-7978-1871)
